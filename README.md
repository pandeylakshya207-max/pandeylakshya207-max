## 🧭 About

I'm a B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) student at Dayananda Sagar University, building at the intersection of applied ML and real-world systems.

I'm an **AI Research Contributor at MIT CSAIL** under Prof. Manolis Kellis (Mantis AI, Class S cohort), where I build AI tutoring tools using LangChain and vector embeddings — including a Socratic AI tutor and a cognitive modeling system that simulates learner mastery over time.

I'm also **Co-Founder & CEO of [EDUING.in](https://eduing.in)**, a unified university admissions platform helping students discover, compare, and apply to universities across India through a single profile — built alongside Aaryan Sharma (COO) and Prince Mittal (CTO).

My first open-source contribution — `AICLContextSelector`, an adaptive context-selection method for RAG pipelines based on ECIR 2025 research — is merged into PyTerrier's RAG library (`terrierteam/pyterrier_rag`). I've since contributed to PyMC-devs as well.

I've competed across India's hackathon circuit (IITs, IIMs, national challenges) — 22+ hackathons, building multi-agent systems for problems ranging from air quality intelligence to emergency response coordination.

## 🧪 Open Source

- **[terrierteam/pyterrier_rag#70](https://github.com/terrierteam/pyterrier_rag/pull/70)** — Implemented `AICLContextSelector`, an adaptive context-selection transformer for RAG pipelines (based on Chandra, Ganguly & Ounis, ECIR 2025). Reduced average context size from a fixed 10 to an adaptive 9.7 documents/query while improving answer recall from 21.2% (k=1) to 51.2% (k=10). Addressed maintainer review across 15 commits.
